I'm overwhelmed trying to learn everything at once

Your Situation

There's so much to learn — prayer, Arabic, fiqh, the Quran — and it feels like you need to know it all right now.

What to know

Religion in Islam is explicitly described as easy, not effortless — and even the Quran itself wasn't revealed all at once.

What you can do

  • Hold onto this directly: 'The religion is ease... so take a middle course, if you can't be perfect, then come close' (Sahih al-Bukhari 39).
  • Notice the precedent: the Quran itself was revealed gradually over roughly 23 years, not all at once — learning your deen gradually follows the same pattern the revelation itself did.
  • Pick one thing at a time. Learning to pray one prayer well, or memorizing one short surah, is real progress — it doesn't need to compete with everything else you haven't learned yet.
Sources: Sahih al-Bukhari 39
