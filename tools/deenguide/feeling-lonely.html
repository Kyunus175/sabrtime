I feel completely alone

Your Situation

Whether or not there are people around you, you feel genuinely alone — unseen, unheard, or disconnected.

What to know

The Quran describes Allah's closeness in terms stronger than physical company — not just present nearby, but aware of what you haven't even said out loud.

What you can do

  • Sit with this verse: 'We are closer to him than his jugular vein' (Surah Qaf 50:16) — describing Allah's knowledge of you as nearer than your own pulse.
  • Remember: 'He is with you wherever you are' (Surah Al-Hadid 57:4) — not a promise that people will always be around, but that you are never actually unaccompanied.
  • Loneliness and worship aren't opposites — dhikr, dua, and prayer are all ways of turning toward Someone who's already turned toward you.
Sources: Surah Qaf 50:16 · Surah Al-Hadid 57:4
