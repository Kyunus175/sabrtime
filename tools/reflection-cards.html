What are Reflection Cards?

Pick a short reflection, choose a background that fits its mood, and export a ready-to-share 1080×1920 image for Instagram Stories, WhatsApp Status, or anywhere else. Everything happens on your device — nothing is uploaded, and no account is needed.

How it works

  1. Pick a reflection from a topic, or tap ✨ Create Random Reflection.
  2. Tap a thumbnail under 🎨 Background, or use Previous / Random / Next.
  3. Tap ✏️ Edit Text to adjust the wording — nothing changes automatically.
  4. Tap 📤 Share Card or ⬇️ Save to Device to export a 1080×1920 image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is any of my data sent to a server?

No. The card is drawn entirely in your browser. Saved and recent cards are stored only on your device using local storage — DeenGuide never sees them.

Do I need to log in?

No account or login is required for any part of Reflection Cards.

Are these Quran verses or hadith?

No. Reflection Cards are short, original reflections meant for encouragement — not scripture and not a fatwa. For sourced guidance, use DeenGuide.

What size are the exported images?

1080×1920 pixels — the standard size for Instagram Stories, WhatsApp Status, and Facebook Stories.
