Qibla Direction in Houston, TX: Exact Angle & How to Find It

From Houston, Qibla points northeast — not the direction a flat map might suggest. Here's the exact bearing and how to verify it wherever you are in the city.

📖 4 min read 🕋 Qibla 🗓️ Updated July 21, 2026
Quick answer: The Qibla direction from Houston, TX is approximately 45.6° from true north — roughly northeast. This holds for the greater Houston area (downtown, Sugar Land, Katy, The Woodlands, Pearland) with only fractional-degree variation across the metro.

Why Northeast, Not East?

Houston sits at roughly 29.76°N, 95.37°W. The Kaaba in Makkah is at 21.42°N, 39.83°E. A straight line drawn on a flat map between the two might suggest facing more directly east — but that's a map-projection illusion, not the real shortest path.

The actual shortest route between two points on a sphere is a great-circle path, and for Houston's position on the globe, that path curves up toward the northeast rather than running due east. This is the same reason Qibla from most of North America points northeast rather than the direction that "looks right" on a Mercator map.

How to Verify It Yourself

  • Compass app: Set your phone to true north (not magnetic) if the option is available, and face 45.6°.
  • Qibla finder tool: A GPS-based tool recalculates this automatically for your exact spot in Houston, removing any manual compass math.
  • Local mosque: Most Houston-area mosques — including those serving the Southwest Houston, Katy, and Northwest communities — already have their prayer direction fixed and verified; if you're praying there, follow the mihrab.
