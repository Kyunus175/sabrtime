Is Qibla East or West? The Answer for Your Country

Qibla is west for South Asia, northeast for North America, southeast for Europe — never one answer for everyone. Here's the direct breakdown by region, and why "always west" is a myth.

The Short Answer

Qibla is the direction of the Kaaba in Makkah, Saudi Arabia — the point every Muslim faces during prayer. But "which direction" only has a real answer once you attach a location to the question. Someone praying in New York, someone in Jakarta, and someone in Cairo are all facing the same building, yet pointing in three completely different compass directions.

Is Qibla East or West?

This is the single most-searched way people ask this question, and the honest answer is: it depends on where you are — Qibla is not fixed to "east" or "west" for the whole world. Here's the direct breakdown by region:

  • India, Pakistan, Bangladesh: Qibla is roughly west, slightly north of due west.
  • United States, Canada: Qibla is roughly northeast — not west, and not directly east either.
  • United Kingdom, most of Europe: Qibla is roughly southeast.
  • Indonesia, Malaysia, Southeast Asia: Qibla is roughly northwest.
  • China, East Asia: Qibla is roughly west, tilting north.

So if you've searched "is Qibla east or west" and landed here — the real answer is that both are correct somewhere in the world, and neither is correct everywhere. Your specific country and city determine which one applies to you, and even within one country the angle shifts as you move further east or west.

Why Qibla Isn't a Fixed Direction

The Kaaba is one specific point on the globe: roughly 21.4°N, 39.8°E. Every other point on Earth sits somewhere around it — north, south, east, west, or some angle in between. Qibla is simply the bearing that points from your location to that one spot, which means it's different almost everywhere, and changes gradually as you move even a few hundred miles.

Rough Direction by Region

These are general reference points only — always calculate your exact bearing rather than relying on a regional estimate:

  • United States & Canada: generally northeast
  • United Kingdom & Western Europe: generally southeast
  • Turkey & the Levant: generally south
  • Pakistan & North India: generally west
  • Indonesia & Malaysia: generally northwest
  • Australia: generally northwest

The "Qibla Is Always West" Myth

This is one of the most common Qibla misconceptions, and it usually comes from South Asia, where Qibla genuinely is roughly west. But that's a coincidence of geography, not a rule. Move to North America and it flips to northeast; move to Turkey and it's south. There's no single compass direction that's correct for the whole Muslim world.

Rule of thumb: if an app or website gives you one universal direction without asking for your location, it's wrong. Correct Qibla always depends on where you are standing right now.

How Qibla Direction Is Actually Calculated

Qibla is worked out using great-circle geometry — the shortest path between two points on a sphere — between your coordinates and the Kaaba's. This is why the direction can look strange on a standard flat map, where straight lines distort the true shortest path across a curved Earth. Why this trips people up

Most people expect Qibla to behave like "east" or "west" — a single word that works everywhere. It doesn't. Treating it as a location-specific calculation, rather than a fixed compass point, is the single biggest shift in getting it right.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which direction is Qibla?
Qibla is the direction of the Kaaba in Makkah. It's not a single compass direction worldwide — it depends on where you're standing. In the US it's generally northeast, in most of Europe it's southeast, and in parts of East Asia it's west. The only way to know your exact angle is to calculate it from your specific location.
Is Qibla always west?
No. Qibla is only roughly west for people east of Makkah, such as in parts of South and East Asia. For most of North America it's northeast, and for much of Europe it's southeast. Assuming "west" from anywhere is one of the most common Qibla mistakes.
What direction do Muslims pray in?
Muslims pray facing the Kaaba in Makkah, Saudi Arabia. This is called the Qibla direction. The exact compass bearing changes depending on where in the world you're located.
How is Qibla direction determined?
Qibla is calculated using great-circle geometry between your exact latitude and longitude and the coordinates of the Kaaba (21.4225° N, 39.8262° E). This gives the shortest path across the globe, which is why the direction can look surprising on a flat map.
Is Qibla east or west?
Neither, universally. Qibla is roughly west for South Asia, roughly northeast for North America, roughly southeast for Europe, and other directions elsewhere — it genuinely depends on where you stand. There is no single "east or west" answer that applies to the whole world.
Is Qibla in the west in India?
For most of India, Qibla is roughly west, slightly north of due west, since Makkah lies west and somewhat north of the Indian subcontinent. The exact angle still shifts by several degrees between, for example, Mumbai and Kolkata.
What direction is namaz prayed in?
Namaz (salah) is prayed facing the Qibla, the direction of the Kaaba in Makkah. The compass direction for this varies by location — it is not fixed to any single cardinal direction worldwide.
