Qibla Direction in Dearborn, MI: Exact Angle & How to Find It

Home to one of the largest Muslim communities in the United States — here's the exact Qibla bearing for Dearborn, and how to verify it wherever you are in the city.

📖 4 min read 🕋 Qibla 🗓️ Updated July 23, 2026
Quick answer: The Qibla direction from Dearborn, MI is approximately 51.9° from true north — northeast. This holds across the Dearborn area (East Dearborn, West Dearborn, Dearborn Heights) with only fractional-degree variation.

Why Northeast?

Dearborn sits at roughly 42.32°N, 83.18°W. The Kaaba in Makkah is at 21.42°N, 39.83°E. As with most of North America, the shortest path between the two isn't the direction a flat map might suggest — it's a great-circle route that curves up toward the northeast, roughly 52° from true north.

How to Verify It Yourself

A Community With Deep Roots

Dearborn holds one of the highest concentrations of Arab-American and Muslim residents anywhere in the United States, shaped by over a century of Middle Eastern immigration. The city is home to numerous mosques, Islamic schools, and community institutions — making accurate Qibla direction and prayer times a genuinely practical daily need for a large share of local residents.
