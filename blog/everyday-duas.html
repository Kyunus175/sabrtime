Everyday Duas: Bathroom, Home, Eating & Sleep

Short, authentic duas for the moments that repeat every single day — entering and leaving the bathroom, coming home, eating, and going to sleep. Each one is only a line or two, and every line is sourced.

Entering & Leaving the Bathroom

These two are among the shortest duas in daily use, and among the most consistently practiced — reported directly from how the Prophet ﷺ himself moved through his day.

Before entering
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْخُبُثِ وَالْخَبَائِثِ
Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-khubthi wal-khaba'ith
"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from male and female devils."
Sahih al-Bukhari 142, Sahih Muslim 375
Enter with the left foot. This is the exact wording narrated by Anas ibn Malik (RA) describing what the Prophet ﷺ would say every time.
After leaving
غُفْرَانَكَ
Ghufranak
"Your forgiveness [I seek]."
Jami' at-Tirmidhi 7, Sunan Abi Dawud 30
That's the complete dua — a single word. Narrated by Aishah (RA), who said this is what the Prophet ﷺ would say every time he came out.

Entering & Leaving Home

Both duas frame the home as a place that opens and closes in Allah's name, not just your own comings and goings.

Entering home
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَلَجْنَا، وَبِسْمِ اللَّهِ خَرَجْنَا، وَعَلَى رَبِّنَا تَوَكَّلْنَا
Bismillahi walajna, wa bismillahi kharajna, wa 'ala Rabbina tawakkalna
"In the name of Allah we enter, in the name of Allah we leave, and upon our Lord we place our trust."
Sunan Abi Dawud 5096
Follow it by greeting your household with Salam — even an empty house, since it's still Sunnah to greet it.
Leaving home
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ
Bismillahi tawakkaltu 'ala Allahi wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah
"In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah, and there is no might nor power except with Allah."
Sunan Abi Dawud 5095, Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3426
The Prophet ﷺ said whoever recites this on leaving is told: "You are guided, defended, and protected" — and the shayateen move away from them.

Before & After Eating

The "before" dua is one word most Muslims already say without thinking — the "after" version is worth learning properly.

Before eating
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ
Bismillah
"In the name of Allah."
Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim
If you forget and only remember partway through, say "Bismillahi awwalahu wa akhirahu" — "In the name of Allah at its beginning and its end." (Sunan Abi Dawud 3767, Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1858)
After eating
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنِي هَذَا وَرَزَقَنِيهِ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِنِّي وَلَا قُوَّةٍ
Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi at'amani hadha wa razaqanihi min ghayri hawlin minni wa la quwwah
"Praise be to Allah who fed me this and provided it for me, without any might or power on my part."
Sunan Abi Dawud 4023, Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3458

Before Sleeping & Upon Waking

Two short lines that frame sleep as a small return to Allah, and waking as a small resurrection.

Before sleeping
بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا
Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya
"In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live."
Sahih al-Bukhari 6324
Upon waking
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ
Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi ahyana ba'da ma amatana wa ilayhin-nushur
"Praise be to Allah who gave us life after having taken it from us, and unto Him is the resurrection."
Sahih al-Bukhari 6312

Why Bother With Duas This Small?

None of these take more than a few seconds. That's the point — they're not meant to be a separate devotional practice you schedule time for, but a way of keeping ordinary, repeated moments connected to remembrance rather than running on autopilot. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dua for entering the bathroom?
"Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-khubthi wal-khaba'ith" — "O Allah, I seek refuge in You from male and female devils." It's recited before entering, ideally stepping in with the left foot. (Sahih al-Bukhari 142, Sahih Muslim 375)
What do you say after leaving the bathroom?
"Ghufranak" — "Your forgiveness [I seek]." This is the entire dua; short, and reported to have been said by the Prophet ﷺ every time he left. (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 7, Sunan Abi Dawud 30)
What is the dua for entering the house?
"Bismillahi walajna, wa bismillahi kharajna, wa 'ala Rabbina tawakkalna" — "In the name of Allah we enter, in the name of Allah we leave, and upon our Lord we place our trust." (Sunan Abi Dawud 5096)
Do you say Bismillah before every meal, even a snack?
Yes — saying Bismillah applies to any food or drink, not just full meals. If you forget and remember partway through, the recommended dua is "Bismillahi awwalahu wa akhirahu" (In the name of Allah at its beginning and its end).
What is the dua before sleeping?
"Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya" — "In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live." Recited while lying down, before closing your eyes. (Sahih al-Bukhari 6324)

Ten small lines, spread across a single ordinary day — bathroom, doorway, table, bed. None of them ask for more than a moment, and that's exactly why they're worth keeping.
