Sources & Methodology

Every answer in DeenGuide is built from the Quran and authentic hadith collections — mainly Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim — with the exact reference shown so you can look it up yourself. No source, hadith number, or scholarly opinion is invented.

  • Islamic scholarship contains legitimate differences of opinion. Where a genuine difference exists, DeenGuide says so rather than presenting one view as the only one.
  • DeenGuide does not follow one single madhhab (school of jurisprudence) — where views differ by madhhab, that's noted directly.
  • For personal, high-stakes, or complicated situations, DeenGuide points you toward a qualified local scholar rather than issuing a final ruling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DeenGuide a fatwa service?

No. It gives general, sourced educational guidance — not a personal religious ruling. For your specific situation, consult a qualified local scholar.

What sources does DeenGuide use?

The Quran and authentic hadith collections such as Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim, with the reference shown under each answer.

What if scholars have different opinions on my situation?

DeenGuide notes when a genuine difference of opinion exists among scholars, rather than presenting a single view as universally agreed upon.

Should I consult a scholar instead of using DeenGuide?

For anything personal, high-stakes, or dependent on your specific circumstances — yes. DeenGuide is a starting point, not a final personal ruling.
