About SabrTime

A free Islamic companion built on one idea: worship tools shouldn't come with ads, logins, or a price tag.

What SabrTime Is

SabrTime is a free web app that brings together the everyday tools of Muslim worship in one calm, uncluttered place — prayer times, a Qibla compass, a digital tasbeeh counter, a dua library, dhikr habit tracking, and an Islamic calendar. It works directly in your browser, installs to your home screen like a native app, and needs no account to get started.

Why It's Free

Core worship features — prayer times, Qibla, duas, tasbeeh, and basic tracking — are free and will stay free. SabrTime doesn't run ads and doesn't sell user data. Where the app does accept support (optional donations, an optional Pro tier for extra personalization features), that support goes toward covering hosting, APIs, and continued development — never toward gatekeeping the essentials.

Privacy First

Most of what you do in SabrTime — your streaks, your tasbeeh counts, your saved duas — stays on your own device rather than being uploaded to a server. Read the full details in the Privacy Policy.

Who's Behind It

SabrTime is built and maintained by Yunus Khan, a solo developer working on this as a long-term project for the Muslim community — not a company, not a startup with investors, just one person trying to build something genuinely useful.
