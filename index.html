أَصْبَحْنَا وَأَصْبَحَ الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ
"We have entered the morning and the dominion belongs to Allah."
— Abu Dawud
اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا وَبِكَ نَحْيَا وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ
"O Allah, by You we enter morning, by You we enter evening, by You we live and by You we die."
— Tirmidhi
رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي
"My Lord, expand my chest and ease my task for me."
— Surah Ta-Ha 20:25–26
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا
"O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, good provision and accepted deeds."
— Ibn Majah
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ
"In the name of Allah with whose name nothing on earth or in heaven can cause harm."
— Abu Dawud
اللَّهُمَّ أَنتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنتَ خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ
"O Allah, You are my Lord. There is no god but You. You created me and I am Your servant."
— Bukhari — Sayyid al-Istighfar
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَصْبَحْتُ مِنكَ فِي نِعْمَةٍ وَعَافِيَةٍ وَسِتْرٍ
"O Allah, I have entered the morning blessed with Your favour, wellbeing and protection."
— Abu Dawud
اللَّهُمَّ مَا أَصْبَحَ بِي مِن نِّعْمَةٍ فَمِنكَ وَحْدَكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
"O Allah, whatever blessing I have this morning is from You alone, You have no partner."
— Abu Dawud
اللَّهُمَّ عَافِنِي فِي يَوْمِي هَذَا
"O Allah, grant me wellbeing in this day of mine."
— Abu Dawud
أَصْبَحْنَا عَلَى فِطْرَةِ الْإِسْلَامِ وَعَلَى كَلِمَةِ الْإِخْلَاصِ
"We have entered the morning upon the natural disposition of Islam and the word of sincerity."
— Ahmad

"We have entered the evening and the dominion belongs to Allah."
— Muslim
اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا وَبِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا وَبِكَ نَحْيَا
"O Allah, by You we enter evening, by You we enter morning, and by You we live."
— Tirmidhi

— Muslim

"O Allah, grant me health in my body, my hearing, and my sight."
— Abu Dawud
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ
"O Allah, I ask You for pardon and wellbeing in this world and the Hereafter."
— Abu Dawud
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَمْسَيْتُ مِنكَ فِي نِعْمَةٍ وَعَافِيَةٍ وَسِتْرٍ
"O Allah, I have entered the evening blessed with Your favour, wellbeing and protection."
— Abu Dawud
اللَّهُمَّ مَا أَمْسَى بِي مِن نِّعْمَةٍ فَمِنكَ وَحْدَكَ
"O Allah, whatever blessing I have this evening is from You alone."
— Abu Dawud
اللَّهُمَّ قِنِي عَذَابَكَ يَوْمَ تَبْعَثُ عِبَادَكَ
"O Allah, protect me from Your punishment on the Day You resurrect Your servants."
— Abu Dawud
بِسْمِكَ رَبِّي وَضَعْتُ جَنبِي وَبِكَ أَرْفَعُهُ
"In Your name, my Lord, I lie down, and in Your name I rise."
— Bukhari
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْكَسَلِ وَالْهَرَمِ
"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from laziness and old age weakness."
— Bukhari
حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ
"Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best disposer of affairs."
— Surah Al-Imran 3:173
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ
"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief."
— Bukhari
لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ
"There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers."
— Surah Al-Anbiya 21:87
اللَّهُمَّ لَا سَهْلَ إِلَّا مَا جَعَلْتَهُ سَهْلًا وَأَنتَ تَجْعَلُ الْحَزْنَ سَهْلًا
"O Allah, there is no ease except what You make easy, and You make what is difficult easy."
— Ibn Hibban
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ
"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from anxiety, sorrow, weakness, and laziness."
— Bukhari

"Verily, with hardship comes ease."
— Surah Ash-Sharh 94:6

"O Ever-Living, O Sustainer, by Your mercy I seek relief."
— Tirmidhi
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْفَقْرِ وَالْقِلَّةِ وَالذِّلَّةِ
"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from poverty, scarcity, and humiliation."
— Abu Dawud

"Unquestionably, the allies of Allah — there will be no fear concerning them, nor will they grieve."
— Surah Yunus 10:62

"For indeed, with hardship will be ease. Indeed, with hardship will be ease."
— Surah Ash-Sharh 94:5–6
حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ
"Sufficient for me is Allah; there is no deity except Him. On Him I have relied."
— Surah At-Tawbah 9:129
وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ
"And whoever relies upon Allah — then He is sufficient for him."
— Surah At-Talaq 65:3
اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي
"O Allah, You are Forgiving and love forgiveness, so forgive me."
— Tirmidhi

"My Lord, forgive me and accept my repentance. You are the Accepting of repentance, the Merciful."
— Ibn Majah

"I seek forgiveness from Allah, other than Whom there is no god, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer."
— Abu Dawud

"My Lord, I have wronged myself, so forgive me."
— Surah Al-Qasas 28:16
رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لَنَا وَلِإِخْوَانِنَا الَّذِينَ سَبَقُونَا بِالْإِيمَانِ
"Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith."

— Surah Al-Muminun 23:118

"O Allah, forgive me my sins, my ignorance and my excesses in my affairs."
— Bukhari

— Muslim

"All praise is for Allah, an abundant, good and blessed praise."

"Praise be to Allah who gave us life after causing us to die, and to Him is the resurrection."
— Bukhari

"O Allah, to You belongs all praise; You are the Light of the heavens and the earth."
— Bukhari

"O Allah, to You belongs all praise as befits the glory of Your face."
— Ibn Majah
رَبَّنَا لَكَ الْحَمْدُ مِلءَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمِلءَ الْأَرْضِ
"Our Lord, to You belongs praise filling the heavens and the earth."
— Muslim
أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ
"I seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Shaytan."
— Quran
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ
"In the name of Allah, I put my trust in Allah, and there is no power or strength except with Allah."
— Abu Dawud
حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ
"Sufficient for me is Allah; there is no deity except Him. On Him I have relied."

اللَّهُمَّ احْفَظْنِي مِن بَيْنِ يَدَيَّ وَمِنْ خَلْفِي وَعَن يَمِينِي وَعَن شِمَالِي
"O Allah, protect me from in front and from behind, from my right and from my left."
— Abu Dawud
آيَةُ الْكُرْسِيِّ — اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ
"Allah — there is no deity except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of existence." (Ayatul Kursi)
— Surah Al-Baqarah 2:255

— Surah Al-Falaq 113:1–2
