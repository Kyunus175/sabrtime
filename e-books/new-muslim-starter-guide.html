About This Book

The New Muslim Starter Guide is written for the very beginning — for anyone who has recently accepted Islam, or who is still finding their footing in early practice. It doesn't try to teach everything at once. Instead, it's built to be read a page at a time, in short, honest sections that answer the questions new Muslims most often carry quietly: Am I doing enough? Do I need to know Arabic first? What happens when I make a mistake?

Every chapter is grounded in the Quran and authentic hadith, written in a warm, judgment-free tone — because starting is hard enough without also feeling like you're behind.

A note on sourcing

Every claim in this guide is grounded in the Quran or an authentic hadith, cited with its source. Where a matter has genuine scholarly disagreement, the guide stays general rather than presenting one view as absolute.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is The New Muslim Starter Guide free?
Yes, completely free. No email, account, or payment is required to download it.
Who is this guide for?
Anyone who has recently accepted Islam or is still finding their footing in early practice — reverts, converts, or anyone who wants a gentle, judgment-free starting point.
Do I need to know Arabic to use this guide?
No. The guide is written entirely in English and explains that Arabic for prayer is learned gradually — it's never a prerequisite to starting.
Can I read this offline?
Yes. Once downloaded, the PDF is yours to keep and read anytime, with no internet connection needed.
Can I share this guide with someone who just accepted Islam?
Yes, freely. This guide is meant to be shared — pass it on to anyone who might find it useful.
