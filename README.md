# 🕌 SabrTime — Peace for Your Heart

> **"Be patient — and your patience is only through Allah."** — Quran 16:127

SabrTime is a **free Islamic web app** built to help Muslims find peace, calm and closeness to Allah through daily duas, dhikr, and reflection. No downloads. No ads. Pure sadaqah jariyah. 🤲

🔗 **Live App:** [kyunus175.github.io/sabrtime](https://kyunus175.github.io/sabrtime)

---

## ✨ Features

### 📿 Daily Dua
- Displays a beautiful Arabic dua with full translation and source reference
- Category tag shows what type of dua it is (Morning, Forgiveness, etc.)
- **↻ Next Dua** button to cycle through the entire duas library
- **💚 Share on WhatsApp** — opens WhatsApp directly with formatted dua text

### 📖 Duas Library (100+)
- Full searchable library of **100+ authentic duas** from Quran and Hadith
- **10 categories** to filter by:
  - 🌅 Morning
  - 🌆 Evening
  - 💭 Anxiety & Stress
  - 🤲 Forgiveness
  - 🌿 Health & Healing
  - ✨ Gratitude
  - 🛡️ Protection
  - 👨‍👩‍👧 Family
  - ✈️ Travel
  - 📚 Knowledge
- Every dua has Arabic text, English translation, and hadith/Quran source
- Individual **💚 Share on WhatsApp** button on every single dua

### 🌿 Mood-Based Duas
- Select how your heart feels today from **8 moods:**
  - 😔 Stressed · 😢 Sad · 😊 Happy · 😡 Angry
  - 😨 Fear · 😰 Anxiety · 🤒 Sick · 🤲 Forgiveness
- App instantly shows the perfect Arabic dua for your current emotional state

### 🤲 Personal Tasbeeh Counter
- Large circular tap button for easy dhikr counting
- Ripple animation on every tap
- **+ Add Count** and **↺ Reset** buttons
- Count displayed in large beautiful Amiri font

### 🌍 Global Community Tasbeeh
- Contribute your personal tasbeeh count to a global community counter
- Count is **saved in localStorage** — persists across sessions
- Live pulsing dot indicator
- See the collective remembrances of the ummah

### ⏳ Sabr Reflection Timer
- 60-second guided reflection session
- Beautiful animated SVG circular progress ring (gold → teal gradient)
- Tracks number of **reflections completed**
- Start and Reset controls

### 💰 Support SabrTime (Donation)
- **☕ Buy Me a Coffee** — one-time support
- **💛 PayPal Donate** — direct donation
- Built-in Arabic ayah about giving sadaqah
- App will always remain **free for everyone**
